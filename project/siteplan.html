Bolivia Adventure Tours - Site Plan

Purpose

Bolivia Adventure Tours is a tourism website designed to promote and facilitate booking of guided tour packages to Bolivia's most iconic destinations. The site targets adventure travelers, cultural enthusiasts, and nature lovers aged 25-65 with disposable income for travel experiences.

Target Audience

  • Adventure travelers seeking authentic experiences
  • Cultural tourists interested in indigenous communities
  • Nature enthusiasts and wildlife lovers
  • Age range: 25-65 years old
  • International travelers from North America, Europe, and developed Asia-Pacific regions
  • Moderate to high income level

Design Elements

Color Palette

Primary Green #1a472a
Secondary Green #2a5f3d
Accent Gold #d4af37
Light Gray #f4f4f4
White #ffffff

Typography

  • Primary Font: Arial, sans-serif (accessible, professional)
  • Heading sizes: H1 (36px), H2 (28px), H3 (22px)
  • Body text: 16px line-height 1.6 for readability

  • Dynamic testimonials carousel
