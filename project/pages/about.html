About Bolivia Adventure Tours

Your gateway to authentic Bolivian experiences

Our Story

Bolivia Adventure Tours was founded in 2010 by a group of passionate travel enthusiasts and native Bolivians who wanted to share the incredible beauty and cultural richness of their country with the world. What began as small group tours has grown into a respected tourism company operating across Bolivia's most iconic destinations.

We believe that tourism should benefit both travelers and local communities. Every tour supports local guides, artisans, and businesses, ensuring that your adventure makes a positive impact.

Our Mission

To provide transformative travel experiences that showcase Bolivia's natural wonders and rich indigenous cultures while promoting sustainable tourism practices and supporting local communities.

Sustainability

Protecting Bolivia's environment for future generations through responsible tourism practices.

Community

Authenticity

Our Team

Our team consists of experienced travel professionals, certified guides, and local experts with deep knowledge of Bolivia's geography, history, and culture. All team members undergo extensive training in safety, environmental conservation, and cultural sensitivity.

Why Choose Us?

  • Local Expertise: Our guides are born and raised in Bolivia with intimate knowledge of every destination
  • Safety First: All tours meet international safety standards with certified equipment and comprehensive insurance
  • Customization: We tailor itineraries to match your interests, fitness level, and travel style
  • Sustainable Practices: We minimize environmental impact and support conservation initiatives
  • Community Support: 20% of profits are reinvested in local communities and education programs
  • Small Groups: Limited group sizes ensure personalized attention and better experiences
  • Competitive Prices: We offer excellent value without compromising quality or ethics
  • Award-Winning Service: Recognized by international tourism organizations for excellence
