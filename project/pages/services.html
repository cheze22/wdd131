Salar de Uyuni - 3 Days

$450 USD per person

Experience the world's largest salt flat at 10,582 square kilometers. During the dry season, marvel at geometric salt patterns. In the rainy season (December-March), witness the stunning mirror effect as water reflects the sky.

Highlights:

  • Visit the Train Cemetery with vintage locomotives
  • Explore the Incahuasi Island with giant cacti
  • Watch sunset over the infinite white landscape
  • Stargazing experience (minimal light pollution)
  • Traditional dinner with local community

Included:

  • Professional English-speaking guide
  • All transportation
  • Meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
  • Accommodation in basic lodges

Amazon Rainforest Adventure - 4 Days

$620 USD per person

Journey into one of Earth's most biodiverse regions. The Bolivian Amazon covers over 48% of the country's territory. Spot wildlife including jaguars, anacondas, river dolphins, and over 800 bird species.

Highlights:

  • Jungle canopy walks at Madidi National Park
  • Pink river dolphin spotting on the Amazon River
  • Indigenous community visits and cultural exchange
  • Night jungle expeditions for nocturnal wildlife
  • Piranha fishing experience

Included:

  • Expert naturalist guide
  • Jungle lodge accommodation
  • All meals with local cuisine
  • Canoe and hiking equipment

Lake Titicaca & Cultural Experience - 2 Days

$280 USD per person

At 3,812 meters above sea level, Lake Titicaca is the world's highest navigable lake. Visit the remarkable Uros floating islands built entirely from totora reeds and experience authentic indigenous Aymara culture.

Highlights:

  • Visit floating islands of the Uros people
  • Learn traditional totora reed boat building
  • Explore Copacabana colonial town
  • Visit Tiwanaku archaeological site
  • Home-cooked meal with local families

Included:

  • Cultural guide fluent in local languages
  • Boat transportation
  • Hotel accommodation in Copacabana
  • Meals at local restaurants

Potosí Mining & History Tour - 1 Day

$95 USD per person

Explore Potosí, once the world's richest city, home to the famous Cerro Rico silver mine. Learn about Bolivia's colonial history and the miners who have shaped the nation. A sobering yet fascinating journey.

Highlights:

  • Visit active silver mines with safety equipment
  • Meet miners and hear their stories
  • Explore the Mining Museum
  • Colonial architecture tour of the city
  • Visit traditional markets

Included:

  • Professional mining guide
  • Safety equipment and helmets
  • Lunch with traditional miners' food
  • Hotel pickup and dropoff
